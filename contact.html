Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

❓

How do I book an appointment?

You can book online through our website, call us directly, or send a WhatsApp message. We'll confirm your appointment within 24 hours.

💳

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, all major credit/debit cards, UPI payments, and also offer EMI options for expensive treatments.

🏥

Do you accept insurance?

Yes, we work with most major insurance providers. Please bring your insurance card and we'll help with the paperwork.

🚨

What if I have a dental emergency?

Call our emergency line immediately at +91 9876543211. We provide 24/7 emergency services for urgent dental issues.

🚗

Is parking available?

Yes, we have free parking available for patients. Our clinic is also accessible by public transport.

📋

What should I bring for my first visit?

Please bring a valid ID, insurance card (if applicable), any previous dental records, and a list of current medications.
