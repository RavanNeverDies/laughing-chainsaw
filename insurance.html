Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about insurance and payments

❓

What if my insurance doesn't cover everything?

We'll clearly explain what's covered and offer payment plans for the remaining balance. We'll always discuss costs before treatment.

❓

How do I know if I'm eligible for EMI?

Most patients qualify for our EMI plans. Basic requirements are a valid ID, bank account, and treatments above ₹5,000.

❓

Are there any hidden charges?

Absolutely not! We believe in transparent pricing. All costs are discussed upfront before any treatment begins.

❓

Can I combine insurance with EMI?

Yes! You can use insurance for covered portions and opt for EMI on the remaining balance.
