Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about virtual consultations

❓ Is it as good as an in-person visit?

While virtual consultations are excellent for many concerns, some conditions require in-person examination. Our dentist will advise if you need to come in.

❓ Can I get a prescription?

Yes, if medically appropriate, our dentists can prescribe medications electronically after the consultation.

❓ What if I need emergency care?

For true emergencies, please call our emergency hotline or visit the clinic immediately. Virtual consultations are for non-emergency situations.

❓ Is my information secure?

Yes, we use encrypted, HIPAA-compliant video platforms to ensure your privacy and data security.

❓ What's the consultation duration?

Typically 15-20 minutes, but can extend if needed for complex cases.

❓ Can I reschedule?

Yes, you can reschedule up to 2 hours before your appointment time.
